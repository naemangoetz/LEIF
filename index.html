Clinical Context: Integrating LEIF Score with TDM

A high LEIF score should not automatically trigger cessation of infliximab. It serves as a flag for early reassessment. Integrating the score with point-of-care (POC) infliximab drug levels can help differentiate the mechanism of failure and guide subsequent therapy, as proposed below.

Quadrant 1: PK Failure

High Risk + Subtherapeutic IFX Level

Patient is not responding due to insufficient drug exposure from high clearance. Action: Consider accelerated AND intensified infliximab dosing (e.g., 10 mg/kg).

Quadrant 2: PD Failure

High Risk + Therapeutic IFX Level

Patient is not responding despite adequate drug levels, suggesting TNF-independent inflammation. Action: Strongest rationale for an early switch to an alternative MOA (e.g., JAK inhibitor) or timely surgical consultation.

Quadrant 4: The At-Risk Responder

Low Risk + Subtherapeutic IFX Level

Patient is improving, but low drug levels may threaten the durability of the response. Action: Continue IFX but proactively plan for dose optimisation to maintain therapeutic targets.

Quadrant 3: The True Responder

Low Risk + Therapeutic IFX Level

Clinical improvement is aligned with sufficient drug exposure, indicating an effective response. Action: Continue with the standard infliximab protocol with high confidence.
